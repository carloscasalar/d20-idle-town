# An actor's decisions live with the actor, not with the thing decided about

What a thing is and does lives with the thing: a Healing potion's tiers, formula, price, production cost and production time belong to the potion. What an actor needs and chooses to do lives with that actor: which adventurers drink, in what order and which tier is the Company's decision; how many potions to buy is the Company's; how many are made and at what pace is the apothecary's. We decided this so that a decision can later weigh everything its actor knows — a Company choosing between a magic sword and several potions, or drinking less because it has a cleric — without reaching into another module.

## Considered Options

- **One Healing potion module holding the drinking round and the restock plan.** Rejected: it gathers everything about potions in one file, but it puts Company decisions where they cannot see the Company's other signals, and each new kind of item would grow its own copy of those decisions.
