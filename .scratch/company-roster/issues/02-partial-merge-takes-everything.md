# How should supplies be divided when only some survivors join a host?

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

## Current behaviour

`CompanyRoster.merge` (through private `mergeMembers`) in
`src/adventurers/company-roster.ts` moves living adventurers into the
host until its six places are filled. Survivors who do not fit stay in their
original company. The host nevertheless receives that company's entire purse,
all healing potions and the whole stash; those who stay behind retain none of
these shared supplies. Equipped gear stays with each adventurer.

## Why this is questionable

For example, a host of five can take only one of three survivors. All three
survivors' shared supplies go with that one member, leaving the other two
waiting with an empty purse, no potions and no stash. They lose the means to
pay for services or support themselves. It is unclear whether a partial merger
should entail surrendering everything or sharing the original company's resources.

## Possible rules

1. Keep the whole transfer as the price of joining the host, even in a partial merger.
2. Divide gold and potions in proportion to survivors who move, with explicit
   rounding rules, and allocate stash items between the two companies.
3. Transfer shared supplies only when every survivor joins; a partial merger
   moves adventurers and their equipped gear while the remaining company keeps
   its purse, potions and stash.

This issue records a rule question; behaviour remains unchanged in Turn 07c.

### Agent Brief (2026-10-12)

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** When only some survivors of a Company join a host, the host receives a proportional share of that Company's gold, healing potions and stash, rounded down; the rest stays with the survivors who wait.

**Current behavior:**
`CompanyRoster.merge` moves living adventurers of the donor into the host until the host reaches `maxCompanySize`. Survivors who do not fit stay in the donor, which remains an active Company. The host nevertheless receives the donor's whole purse, every healing potion and the whole stash, so those who stay behind keep nothing. Equipped gear stays with each adventurer.

**Desired behavior:**
The owner chose rule 2 of the issue, with whole numbers only. Let `moved` be the living adventurers that join the host and `survivors` the donor's living adventurers before the merger. What goes to the host is always rounded down, and what is not moved stays with the donor:

- Gold: `floor(gold * moved / survivors)` moves, as one coin transfer with the reason used today.
- Healing potions: the same rule applied to each tier separately, on that tier's count.
- Stash: `floor(items * moved / survivors)` items move, taken in the order the stash holds them.

When every survivor joins (`moved` equals `survivors`) the formula moves everything, which is today's behaviour, and the donor is disbanded as today. When nobody moves, nothing moves. Equipped gear, the host's renown rule, who is moved, the fallen and the log lines are unchanged.

No random draw is added, removed or reordered. Results of the default game change wherever a partial merger happens, so the regression hashes are re-recorded once, after the tests below pass.

**Key interfaces:**
- `CompanyRoster.merge` / `CompanyRoster.absorb` — same signatures; only what a partial merger carries changes.
- The healing potion pack of a Company — divided per tier; no fractional count may ever be stored.
- The coin movement of a merger — one transfer of the whole-number share.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A host of five takes one of three survivors of a donor with 100 gp: the host's purse rises by 33 gp and the donor keeps 67 gp.
- [ ] In the same merger, a donor holding 5 basic and 2 greater healing potions gives 1 basic and no greater; it keeps 4 basic and 2 greater.
- [ ] In the same merger, a donor with 3 stash items gives the first one in stash order and keeps the other two; with 2 stash items it gives none.
- [ ] A merger in which every survivor joins moves all the gold, every potion and the whole stash, and the donor is disbanded, exactly as today.
- [ ] After any merger, gold, potion counts per tier and stash sizes of host and donor are whole numbers and add up to the totals before it.
- [ ] Equipped gear moves with its adventurer and the host's renown is the higher of the two, as today.
- [ ] The regression hashes are re-recorded in this change and the report says which tests changed and why; no other seeded expectation is edited without naming it in the report.
