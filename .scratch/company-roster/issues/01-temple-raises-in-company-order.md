# Which fallen adventurers should the temple raise first?

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

## Current behaviour

`CompanyRoster.recruit` in `src/adventurers/company-roster.ts` visits the fallen in company order.
At an operating temple, it pays to raise each adventurer the remaining purse
can afford, skipping anyone whose fee is too high. It does not sort by price
or choose a combination that raises as many adventurers as possible.

## Why this is questionable

Company order determines who returns to life. For example, with 680 gp,
a level-3 adventurer costs 510 gp and two level-1 adventurers cost 190 gp each.
If the level-3 adventurer comes first, only one is raised; raising the two
cheaper adventurers would bring back more companions. Neither the intended
priority nor the significance of company order has been decided.

## Possible rules

1. Keep company order as the priority, skipping unaffordable adventurers.
2. Raise the cheapest first, preserving company order between equal fees, to
   maximise the number raised under the current individual-fee model.
3. Let the company prioritise particular companions, for example by level or
   missing role, even when that raises fewer adventurers.

This issue records a rule question; behaviour remains unchanged in Turn 07c.
