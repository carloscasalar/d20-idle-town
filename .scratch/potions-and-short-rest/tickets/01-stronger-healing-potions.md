# Stronger healing potions in D&D 2024

Type: research (wayfinder:research)
State: closed
Claimed-by: —
Blocked-by: —

## Question

Which healing potions beyond the basic Potion of Healing exist in D&D 2024, what do they heal, what rarity are they, and what do they cost?

## Resolution

Four potions exist in the SRD 5.2.1 (basic, greater, superior, supreme) healing 2d4+2, 4d4+4, 8d4+8, 10d4+20 at Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very Rare. Only the basic one has its own price (50 gp). Others are priced by rarity, with a consumables halving footnote that gives 200 / 2,000 / 20,000 gp; the unhalved table would give 400 / 4,000 / 40,000 gp. No SRD guidance ties them to character level. Details: [research/healing-potion-tiers.md](../research/healing-potion-tiers.md).
