# The Reward set-aside ignores Bounties

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

Found by the owner while reviewing [issue 08](08-reward-set-aside.md). Not reachable with the default configuration: `board.rewardSetAsideFor` holds the apothecary alone, and the apothecary never posts a Bounty.

## What happens

The Reward set-aside covers Contracts only. `Board.rewardTreasury` in `src/sim/board.ts` adds up an employer's `open` and `taken` work of kind Contract, and only `generateQuest` in `src/quests/quest.ts` reads the result.

A Bounty is left out on both sides:

- Its reward is not counted as owed when a later Contract of the guild is sized.
- Its own reward is sized from the guild's whole treasury (`Math.min(guild.treasury, assault.rewardPerLevel * lair.level)` in `generateAssault`), whatever the guild already owes.

So with `guild` in the list, the guild can promise its whole treasury in a Bounty and then another 80% of the same treasury in a Contract. The guild also pays upkeep and posts Contracts on its own holdings, so it can go into debt the way the rule is meant to prevent.

Issue 08 asked for Contracts only, and `test/reward-set-aside.test.ts` pins that ("sets aside Contracts only, even when a configured employer also posts a Bounty"). This is a gap in the rule as specified, not a defect of that slice.

## What it should do

The owner's decision: the set-aside applies to both kinds of work. For an employer in the list, the gold promised in its `open` and `taken` Bounties is owed as well, and a new Bounty is sized from the treasury minus what is owed.

To settle before building:

- Whether a Bounty then takes the whole reduced treasury, as today, or `quests.treasuryShare` of it, as a Contract does.
- `CONTEXT.md` defines the Reward set-aside over Contracts; the entry changes with the rule.
- The existing Contracts-only test is replaced.
- Putting `guild` in the default list is a separate decision and needs its own long-run measurement.

### Agent Brief (2026-10-12)

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** For an employer in the Reward set-aside list, Bounties count as rewards owed and a new Bounty is sized from the treasury left after the rewards owed.

**Current behavior:**
The Reward set-aside covers Contracts only. For an employer named in `board.rewardSetAsideFor`, the Board subtracts the rewards of its `open` and `taken` Contracts from the treasury that a new Contract reward is sized from. A Bounty is ignored on both sides: its reward is not counted as owed, and its own reward is `kinds.assault.rewardPerLevel` times the lair's level, capped by the guild's whole treasury. The guild posts a Bounty whenever its treasury meets `world.postingThreshold`. The default list holds `apothecary` alone, which never posts a Bounty, so the default game does not reach this.

**Desired behavior:**
The owner's three decisions, in this order of precedence over the "To settle before building" list in the issue body:

1. The rewards owed by a listed employer are the rewards of all its `open` and `taken` work, Contracts and Bounties alike. Both a new Contract and a new Bounty are sized from the treasury minus that amount. The amount is released when the work stops being `open` or `taken`.
2. A listed employer's new Bounty is `rewardPerLevel` times the lair's level, capped by `quests.treasuryShare` of the treasury left after the rewards owed (rounded down, as a Contract's cap is). `quests.minimumReward` does not apply to a Bounty.
3. A listed employer does not post a Bounty while the treasury left after the rewards owed is below `world.postingThreshold`. It posts it at a later hour, once that amount meets the threshold. No Bounty is ever posted with a reward of zero or less.

An employer outside the list is unchanged in every respect: its Bounty is capped by its whole treasury, the threshold reads its whole treasury, and Bounties are not counted as owed. With the default configuration the game is identical tick for tick: no random draw is added, removed or reordered, and the regression hashes are not re-recorded.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Board's reward-sizing treasury (today `Board.rewardTreasury` and `WorkContext.rewardTreasury`): counts the employer's Bounties as well as its Contracts.
- Bounty generation (today `generateAssault`): receives the treasury to size from, as `QuestTerms.rewardTreasury` does for a Contract, instead of reading the guild's treasury itself.
- The hourly Bounty posting of the game: for a listed guild, the threshold check reads the treasury left after the rewards owed.
- `board.rewardSetAsideFor`: unchanged shape, validation and default.
