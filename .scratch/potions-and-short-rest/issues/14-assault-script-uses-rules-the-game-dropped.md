# The assault script uses rules the game dropped

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: bug

Found by the architecture review of 2026-10-12. `scripts/assault.ts` estimates how often a Company clears a Bounty. It carries its own short version of what happens between two fights, written before the potions and Short rest work, so its percentages no longer describe the game.

## What it does today

- Between fights it heals every living adventurer for half their maximum hit points, for free. The game no longer has that rule: a Short rest spends Hit Dice.
- It gives the Company four basic potions and never restocks or uses another tier.
- It drinks when hit points are below half the maximum; Bloodied is half or less.
- It turns back when half or fewer of the fighters are left, a line written into the script.
- It imports `COMBAT_RULES`, `QUEST_GENERATION` and `STARTING_GOLD` from `test/helpers/supplied-config`, so a script depends on test code.

## Decided at triage (2026-10-12, owner)

- The script stays, because `scripts/tune.ts` measures whole games and not the outcome of one Bounty by level. It stops carrying rules of its own: it plays the Bounty with the real game.
- No script imports from `test/`. `scripts/calibrate.ts` has the same import and is corrected here too.

### Agent Brief (2026-10-12)

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** The assault script reports how often a Company clears a Bounty using rules the game no longer has. Make it play the Bounty with the real game, and stop scripts importing from the tests.

**Current behavior:**
- `scripts/assault.ts` builds a Lair, a Bounty and a Company by hand and runs the fights itself with `runCombat`. Between fights it heals every living adventurer for half their maximum for free, drinks only basic potions from a fixed pack of four when below half hit points, and turns back when half or fewer of the fighters are left. None of this is what an Expedition does now (Short rest, Hit Dice, the Company's drinking, the turn-back decision, restocking in town).
- It prints one line per pairing of Lair level (5, 6, 8) and Company level (same, or one above): share of Bounties cleared, share that reached the boss, and deaths per attempt. `TRIALS`, `SCALE` and `SEED` come from the environment.
- `scripts/assault.ts` and `scripts/calibrate.ts` import module defaults from `test/helpers/supplied-config`.

**Desired behavior:**
- For each trial the script sets up a game through `Game.forTesting` with one Company of the wanted level and one Lair of the wanted level, and steps the game until that Company's Expedition against the Lair's Bounty has ended (the Lair broken, the Company back in town without breaking it, or the Company wiped). Everything in between is the game's own: taking the Bounty, travel, fights, Short rests, potions, turning back, the return.
- No other Company takes that Bounty or joins the one being measured during a trial.
- The script contains no healing, drinking, retreat or fight loop of its own, and does not call `runCombat`.
- It prints the same three figures for the same pairings, read from what the game exposes (its view, its statistics or its events). `TRIALS` and `SEED` keep their meaning; `SCALE` is the Board's `difficultyScale`.
- No file under `scripts/` imports from `test/`. Scripts take module defaults from the modules that define them.

The game itself does not change: no source file under `src/` needs to change for this. If the game does not expose something the script needs to tell the three outcomes apart or to know the boss was reached, stop and report what is missing instead of reading private state or adding to the game.

**Key interfaces:**
- `Game.forTesting(config, configure)` and `GameScenario` — the setup of each trial (`parties`, `lairs`, `quests`, `town`).
- `Game.step()`, `Game.view()`, `Game.onEvent()` — how a trial is advanced and read.
- `GameConfig` — `board.difficultyScale` for `SCALE`; the roster section if arrivals have to be kept out of a trial.
