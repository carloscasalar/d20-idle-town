# Potion of Healing crafting time and cost in D&D 5e (SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2.1)

Sources, both free official SRD text:

- SRD 5.2.1 (2024 rules): https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf
  (printed page numbers below).
- SRD 5.1 (2014 rules), via the 5thsrd.org mirror:
  https://www.5thsrd.org/adventuring/equipment/tools/ ,
  https://5thsrd.org/adventuring/between_adventures/ ,
  https://www.5thsrd.org/gamemaster_rules/magic_items/potion_of_healing/

## Findings (SRD 5.2.1, the 2024 rules)

Amounts for the three magic tiers are derived by applying the consumable
halving to the Magic Item Crafting Time and Cost table (p. 207); the SRD does
not list them per potion.

| Potion | Time | Raw materials | Tools and proficiency | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Potion of Healing (Common) | 1 day (8 hours of work) | 25 gp | Herbalism Kit, proficiency with it | "Brewing Potions of Healing", p. 103; Herbalism Kit "Craft" line, p. 94 |
| Potion of Healing (greater, Uncommon) | 5 days (10 halved) | 100 gp (200 halved) | Herbalism Kit or Alchemist's Supplies, with proficiency; Arcana proficiency | "Crafting Magic Items", pp. 206-207 |
| Potion of Healing (superior, Rare) | 25 days (50 halved) | 1,000 gp (2,000 halved) | same as above | same |
| Potion of Healing (supreme, Very Rare) | 62.5 days (125 halved) | 10,000 gp (20,000 halved) | same as above | same |

The table in p. 207 lists Common 5 days / 50 gp, Uncommon 10 / 200, Rare 50 /
2,000, Very Rare 125 / 20,000. Its footnote says "The time and cost are halved
for a consumable item other than a Spell Scroll". The basic potion's explicit
rule (1 day, 25 gp) does not equal the halved Common row (2.5 days, 25 gp), so
the dedicated Brewing rule wins for the basic potion.

Details from the same pages:

- Each day of crafting is 8 hours of work; the days "needn't be consecutive"
  (pp. 103, 207).
- One other character can assist, and the time is divided by the number of
  characters working on it; the GM may allow more assistants (p. 207).
- Raw materials: the GM decides availability. Per the SRD, 75% chance in a
  city, 25% elsewhere; if unavailable, wait at least 7 days to check again
  (p. 207).
- The Magic Item Tools table (p. 207) puts "Potion" under "Alchemist's Supplies
  or Herbalism Kit".
- There is no minimum crafter level anywhere in the SRD 5.2.1 crafting rules,
  and a potion of healing needs no prepared spells (spell requirement applies
  only to items that cast spells, p. 207).

## SRD 5.1 (the 2014 rules)

- Herbalism Kit: "proficiency with this kit is required to create antitoxin and
  potions of healing" (5thsrd.org tools page). No time, no raw material cost.
- Downtime "Crafting an Item" (5thsrd.org Between Adventures) covers only
  nonmagical objects: progress in 5 gp increments per day, raw materials worth
  half the market value. No magic item or potion crafting rules.
- The 5.1 potion page gives rarity and healing only; no price and no crafting.
- Not in the free SRD 5.1: any days/gold-by-rarity table, minimum crafter
  level, or the 25 gp / 1 day potion entry. The 2014 numbers live in the paid
  Dungeon Master's Guide and Xanathar's Guide to Everything, which were not read.

## Several potions at once

SRD 5.2.1 words the Brewing rule in the singular ("can create a Potion of
Healing ... over the course of 1 day"), and nothing in pp. 103 or 206-207 lets
one batch yield several potions. The only way to shorten time is more
assistants. The 2014 "Crafting an Item" rule (several items per day while their
total market value is at most 5 gp) does not reach a 50 gp potion.

## NPC apothecary or shop making potions on demand

Not stated. Neither SRD has a rule for hiring an NPC to craft potions on demand.
SRD 5.2.1 says only that Common magic items "can often be bought in a town or
city", Uncommon and Rare "are usually found only in cities", and that rarity
"can help you set prices" (p. 205); the basic potion is a priced gear item,
50 gp (p. 95). The only apothecary mention is a hidden poison stash
("Purchasing Poison"), which is unrelated to healing potions.

## Certain and not certain

Certain (SRD text): the basic potion takes 1 day (8 hours) and 25 gp of raw
material with Herbalism Kit proficiency (2024); the rarity table, the
consumable halving footnote and the Arcana and tool requirements for the other
three tiers.

Derived, not stated: the 5 / 25 / 62.5 day and 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 gp figures
for greater, superior and supreme. They follow from the footnote, but the SRD
gives no rounding rule for the half day and no per-potion figure. Note for
`healing-potion-tiers.md`: its "Magic Item Crafting Cost" line (50 / 200 /
2,000 / 20,000 gp) is the table before the consumable halving; for potions the
raw-material cost is half of that.

Not verified: the printed 2024 Player's Handbook and Dungeon Master's Guide
(the SRD 5.2.1 text appears to reproduce their crafting rules, but the books
themselves were not read), the 2014 DMG and Xanathar's tables, and any
published rule for NPC crafters.
