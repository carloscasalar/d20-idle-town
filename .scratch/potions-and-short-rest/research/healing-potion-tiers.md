# Healing potion tiers in D&D 2024 (SRD 5.2.1)

Source: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf
(printed pages quoted below). First pass by a research subagent; the price
rules were re-read in the SRD text afterwards.

## The four potions (Magic Items A-Z, "Potion, Rarity Varies", p. 236)

| Potion | Healing | Rarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Potion of Healing | 2d4 + 2 | Common |
| Potion of Healing (greater) | 4d4 + 4 | Uncommon |
| Potion of Healing (superior) | 8d4 + 8 | Rare |
| Potion of Healing (supreme) | 10d4 + 20 | Very Rare |

Drinking or administering any potion takes a Bonus Action (p. 204).

## Price

- The basic potion has a fixed price of 50 gp in the Adventuring Gear table (p. 95).
- The other three have no price of their own. Magic items are priced by rarity
  (Magic Item Rarities and Values, p. 206): Common 100, Uncommon 400, Rare 4,000,
  Very Rare 40,000 gp. The table carries the footnote "Halve the value for a
  consumable item other than a Spell Scroll."
- Applying the footnote reproduces the basic potion's 50 gp (Common 100 / 2), so
  the consistent reading gives **50 / 200 / 2,000 / 20,000 gp**. Without the
  footnote the same table would give 100 / 400 / 4,000 / 40,000 gp.
- **Open question for the catalogue ticket:** the player expects the unhalved
  rarity values. The SRD supports the halved ones because of the footnote and
  the 50 gp basic potion; the choice is a game decision, not an SRD fact.
- The SRD says values only "help you set prices", and a seller may ask for a
  service instead of coin.
- Magic Item Crafting Cost (p. 207) lists Common 50, Uncommon 200, Rare 2,000,
  Very Rare 20,000 gp, also with the consumable halving.

## Availability

- No potion-by-level or tier guidance exists in the SRD ("Tiers of Play" has no
  rules attached, pp. 23-24). Any mapping from hero level to potion tier is an
  inference.
- By rarity (p. 205): Common items can often be bought in a town or city;
  Uncommon and Rare are usually found only in cities; rarer ones might be sold
  only in wondrous locations.

## Brewing (out of scope for this map)

A character proficient with the Herbalism Kit can create a basic Potion of
Healing with that kit and 25 gp of raw material over 1 day (8 hours) (p. 103).
