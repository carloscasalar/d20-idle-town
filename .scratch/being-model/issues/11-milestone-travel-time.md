# Milestone travel time

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

How long does travelling from one Milestone to the next take, in Ticks? Decide whether the time per Milestone is the same along the Road (linear) or grows with distance from the town (logarithmic or otherwise), whether it is configurable, and how a Being that is between two Milestones is represented in its state and in Knowledge.

## Comments

From [Tick settlement](03-tick-settlement.md): travel is a lasting Action taken one Tick at a time. A Being's place on the Road is real state, and a Being that breaks off is left where it stands.
