# Knowledge in the slice

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01

## Question

What does each Being in the slice know, and how does it learn it? Decide what a Being sees where it is, what it learns from the Board and from others, how Knowledge goes out of date, and how much of the current game's Job intelligence the slice keeps.

## Comments

From [Tick settlement](03-tick-settlement.md): every Being takes in the world in the first phase of each Tick, before any Mind is consulted. What settlement changes, such as a refusal, a lost Contest or a won Fight, reaches a Mind only in the next Tick, as a failed step or as Knowledge. Decide what each Being takes in at that phase.
