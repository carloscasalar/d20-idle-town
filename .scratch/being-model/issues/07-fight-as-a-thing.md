# Fight as a Thing

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 05

## Question

How does a Fight work as a Thing the world sets up to settle a fight Action? Decide how the combat adapter comes over (ADR 0003: once the drinking round has moved out of it and its encounter description no longer depends on quests/), how each side's Nature and Mind decide between Rounds (who drinks, flee or go on), how the Fight's Rounds fit inside a Tick, and what the Fight leaves behind (dead, loot, a cleared Lair).

## Comments

From [Tick settlement](03-tick-settlement.md): a hostile Action is taken by force and happens unless someone there resists. Decide who may resist and how resisting sets up the Fight. A Fight rolls from its own dice stream, set up with it. Decide how a Fight that lasts longer than one Tick holds the Beings in it.
