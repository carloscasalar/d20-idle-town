# Groups in the slice

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02

## Question

How do the slice's Groups work? Decide how a Party's and a Horde's Nature combine their Members' Needs with their own, how the Group's Mind governs (a Party's way of deciding, a Horde following its leader or Boss), when a Member breaks off from the Group's Plan, what join and leave Actions the slice needs, and what each Member does when a Horde's leader falls.
