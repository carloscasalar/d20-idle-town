Tick settlement — prototype

Throwaway prototype for the wayfinder ticket "Tick settlement". Not part of src/. Built on the resolved "Being contract".

The question. How does the world settle one Tick? Every Being first decides on its own, against the world as it stood when the Tick began. Then the world gathers every Action and settles them in fixed phases: requests go to each Thing's Owner, who answers them all in one consultation. A Contest settles a Thing that has no Owner. Then outcomes are applied and the Things' own rules run. A failed step reaches the Being's Mind at the start of the next Tick, never later in the same one. Lasting Actions such as travel and the Long rest are taken one Tick at a time. Their progress is real state: where the Party stands on the Road, how many hours it has rested.

The run is the slice story, kept small. Grakk's goblins march from their Lair to Mara's Herb garden. Mara's Apothecary posts a Contract on the Board. Red Hand and the Grey Owls both want it.

Placeholders, owned by other tickets: a Milestone takes 3 Ticks ("Milestone travel time"); every Being sees the world as it is ("Knowledge in the slice"); a Fight resolves in one roll inside the Tick ("Fight as a Thing"); a Party is one Being with one Athletics bonus ("Groups in the slice").
