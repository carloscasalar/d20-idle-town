# Should a ruined employer honour taken contracts?

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

Currently, `Game.ruin()` fails only open contracts. A company can finish work
already taken, return after the employer is ruined, and settle normally:
the employer pays the full reward and gains reputation for successful work.
The treasury can fall further below zero despite the employer being ruined.

This is questionable because ruin sells off holdings or dissolves the
organisation, leaving its ability to pay and honour commitments unclear.

Possible rules:

- Honour taken contracts through reserved funds or a guarantor, retaining reputation gains.
- Cancel taken contracts on ruin, define compensation for the company, and award no reputation.
- Allow the work to finish, cap payment at remaining funds, and define whether partial payment earns reputation.

Leave the current behaviour unchanged until the rule is decided.

### Agent Brief (2026-10-12)

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** A Company that completes work taken from an employer that has since been ruined is not paid; it still gains its renown.

**Current behavior:**
When an employer is ruined, only its `open` work is withdrawn from the Board. Work already `taken` goes on. If the Company succeeds and returns after the ruin, settlement runs as for any employer: the full reward moves from the ruined employer's treasury to the Company's purse, the promised item is handed over and the employer gains reputation. An employer is ruined only after days with its treasury below zero and earns nothing afterwards, so that reward is always paid out of debt.

**Desired behavior:**
The owner's decisions. When taken work is settled as a success and its employer is ruined at that moment:

- The Company receives no gold and no promised item reward. The ruined employer's treasury does not move, and its reputation and its counters of completed work do not change.
- The Company gains renown exactly as for paid work, and keeps everything it won on the Expedition itself (loot, experience, a lair's hoard).
- The work ends as completed on the Board and in the game's count of completed work. The gold-paid total does not grow.
- One chronicle line says the Company returned to find the employer ruined and went unpaid. It names the Company, the employer and the reward that was lost.

The rule is the same for a Contract and for a Bounty. Failed work of a ruined employer moves no gold from or to that employer and changes none of its reputation or counters; what the Company itself loses on a failure is as today. Work of an employer that is not ruined at settlement is unchanged in every respect.

No random draw is added, removed or reordered. Results of the default game change wherever this happens, so the regression hashes are re-recorded once, after the tests below pass.

- The Board's settlement of taken work (`Board.settle` and the pay and fail steps of each kind of work) — reads whether the employer is ruined at settlement.
