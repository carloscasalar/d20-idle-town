# Should a ruined employer honour taken contracts?

Status: needs-triage
Category: enhancement

Currently, `Game.ruin()` fails only open contracts. A company can finish work
already taken, return after the employer is ruined, and settle normally:
the employer pays the full reward and gains reputation for successful work.
The treasury can fall further below zero despite the employer being ruined.

This is questionable because ruin sells off holdings or dissolves the
organisation, leaving its ability to pay and honour commitments unclear.

Possible rules:

- Honour taken contracts through reserved funds or a guarantor, retaining reputation gains.
- Cancel taken contracts on ruin, define compensation for the company, and award no reputation.
- Allow the work to finish, cap payment at remaining funds, and define whether partial payment earns reputation.

Leave the current behaviour unchanged until the rule is decided.
