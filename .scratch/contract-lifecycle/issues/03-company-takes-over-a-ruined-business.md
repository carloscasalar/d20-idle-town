# Could a Company take over the business of a ruined employer?

Status: needs-triage
Category: enhancement

Proposed by the owner on 2026-10-12, while triaging [issue 01](01-ruined-employer-still-pays.md).

## The idea

A Company that returns from work it took and finds its employer ruined is not paid (issue 01). Instead of walking away, it could take the business: if it can pay the employer's debt, its members become the new owners.

## Why it is worth looking at

Ruin is permanent today. A ruined service is lost to the town for good; a ruined apothecary means no healing potions for the rest of the game. This would be a way to bring a business back, with a story behind it.

## Why it is not a small change

- Ruin is assumed permanent in the code, the tests and the documents (the potions PRD says "Ruin stays permanent"). Undoing it touches every place that reads `Employer.ruined`.
- A Company cannot own anything. The only precedent is retirement: a veteran leaves the Company, buys a holding and becomes an employer (`CompanyRoster.retire`).
- The chronicle says a ruined employer's holdings are sold off, or that the organisation dissolves.

## Questions to settle

- Who runs the business: one member who leaves the Company, as in retirement, or the Company as a whole?
- What the debt is: the treasury below zero alone, or that plus a price for the business.
- What comes back: the holdings, the service, the reputation, the treasury at zero?
- Which employers can be taken over: any, or only the ones that run a town service?
- Only the Company that was left unpaid, or any Company that can pay?
- How it sits with retirement, which already turns an adventurer into an employer at a fixed price.

Behaviour is unchanged until these are decided.
